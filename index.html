About the practice

Personal care, the way medicine should feel.

At a large group you can feel like a number. Here you see the same provider every visit, someone who learns your history, answers your questions without rushing, and builds a plan around your life.

Legacy Integrative Healthcare blends conventional primary care with attention to nutrition, lifestyle, and long-term wellness, treating the whole person rather than a single symptom. Cynthia Okonkwo founded the practice to give Randallstown families a medical home built on listening, trust, and continuity.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. We are currently welcoming new patients of all ages. Reach out through the contact form or by phone to get started.

What insurance do you take?

We accept most major insurance plans, including Medicare and Medicaid. If you are not sure about your plan, contact us and we will help you check.

Do you offer same-day appointments?

We hold time for same and next-day sick visits whenever possible. Call early in the day for the best availability.

How do telehealth visits work?

After you request a telehealth visit, we connect by secure video at your appointment time. Many common concerns can be handled this way. Emergencies should always go to 911 or the nearest emergency room.

How do I get a prescription refill?

Refills are reviewed based on your history and current treatment. Contact the office or ask your pharmacy to send a refill request, and we will follow up.
